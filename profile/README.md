<h1 align="center">VoiceVibe</h1>

<p align="center"><b>Talk to your computer. It types, speaks & does what you ask.</b></p>

Press a key and say what you need. Dictate, rewrite, chat, generate images,
hear text read aloud, launch agents. Do more without losing your train of
thought. While you speak, choose where it goes: type it, ask the assistant,
ask about what's on your screen, transform selected text, or hand a task to
an AI chat or coding agent.
Voice typing is free and unlimited, and it runs on your device with
Parakeet: 100% offline, no account needed.

**[Download for Windows, macOS and Linux](https://voicevibe.ai/download?utm_source=github&utm_medium=profile)**

## Voice modes

- **Voice Typing** (Free): dictation in any text field, on-device, with Word Replacements
- **Magic Typing**: create or transform text in any app
- **Magic Clipboard**: create or transform text to your clipboard
- **Voice Assistant**: ask questions, chat, and search the web, with spoken answers
- **Text-to-Speech**: read selected text aloud in 120 preset voices, or your own voice clone
- **AI Images**: create new images or edit existing ones
- **Note Taker**: structured notes from speech and files
- **Chat Launcher**: send your words and context to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity and more, ready for you to review and send
- **Agent Launcher**: dictate, review and launch tasks for Claude Code, Codex CLI, Cursor, OpenCode or Pi
- **Add to Dictionary**: teach VoiceVibe your vocabulary

Add context while you speak: selected text, clipboard content, a partial
or full screenshot, or supported files. VoiceVibe is not always
listening: you start every recording with your shortcut.

## Free and Pro

| | Free | Pro | Pro Max |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | $0 | $14.99/month or $143.90/year | $29.99/month or $287.90/year |
| Voice Typing | Unlimited, on-device (Parakeet), 100% offline, 25 languages | Adds cloud transcription cleanup (grammar, filler words, self-corrections); 100 cloud speech languages | Same as Pro |
| Pro modes | – | All of the modes above | Same as Pro, four times the usage allowance |
| Account | Not needed | Required; up to 3 devices | Required; up to 3 devices |

- **Pro modes use cloud AI and need an internet connection.** Pro Voice
  Typing can still work offline with the Local Model or Cloud with Local
  Fallback setting.
- Paid usage is generous but not unlimited; allowances reset monthly.
- **Pro Preview:** try the Pro modes with a small usage allowance, no
  credit card (an account is required; AI Images is not included). Look
  for "Try Pro" in the app.
- No API keys needed, on any plan.
- 7-day money-back guarantee on Pro subscriptions; cancel anytime.

Details: [pricing](https://voicevibe.ai/pricing) · [Free vs Pro](https://voicevibe.ai/comparison)
